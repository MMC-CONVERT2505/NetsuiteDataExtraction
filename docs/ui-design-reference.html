Help

Three steps, every time: connect to a company, choose dates and records, download the file. The run sheet on the left shows where you are.

Vendor payments — money going out to suppliers, including bill payments and their applied bills.

Customer payments — money coming in from customers, and the invoices each payment was applied to.

Deposit applications — customer deposits later applied against invoices.

Journal entries — manual adjustments posted straight to the general ledger, with every line.

If the conversion brief doesn't say, tick all four. Extra records are easier to drop later than to go back for.

The transaction date on the record in NetSuite — not the date it was created or last edited. Both ends are included.

NetSuite's word for a company or location inside one account — for example an Australian entity and a New Zealand entity under the same parent. Leave the box on All companies and locations unless the brief asks for one specific entity.

A full financial year across four record types can take several minutes. The row counts keep climbing while it works — if they've stopped moving for more than five minutes, stop the extract and try a shorter date range.
